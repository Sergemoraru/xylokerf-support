Your workshop. Your information.

This notice describes the XyloQuote app, its support website, and information you choose to provide for support.

Your workshop information

XyloQuote stores the information you enter in the app on your device. This includes your shop name and contact details, currency and pricing defaults, jobs, customer details, scope, estimated and actual costs, private cost notes, quote revisions, acceptance records and reusable templates. Whether you have completed shop setup is also saved locally.

The current app does not create an online account, send your workshop to a publisher-controlled server, or include advertising, tracking or third-party analytics software. The publisher cannot remotely view or restore your local workshop through the app.

Imports, exports and sharing

You choose which XyloKerf CSV and workshop backup files to import. Importing reads those selected files into your local workshop.

When you share a quote PDF, it can include your shop contact details, the customer's details, project scope, work descriptions and quantities, and the quoted price and tax. It excludes internal unit costs, private cost notes, overhead allocation, margin and profit. Review customer-facing descriptions and scope before sharing: text you enter in those fields is included.

A workshop backup contains the full workshop, including customer information, internal costs, notes and settings. Backups are readable JSON files, not password-protected archives. Save and share them only to destinations you choose. The app creates temporary PDF and backup files to support export. These temporary copies may remain in the app's temporary storage until the operating system removes them.

Sharing uses the device's system share tools. If you choose Mail, Messages, Files, iCloud Drive or another service, that service handles the copy according to its own practices. Deleting data in XyloQuote does not remove copies you already exported or sent.

Device backup and retention

Your workshop remains in the app until you delete records or remove the app and its local data. Offloading an app may retain its data. Device or computer backups may also contain app data depending on your system settings; XyloQuote does not provide automatic cross-device synchronization.

You can delete jobs and templates in the app and edit your shop profile. Manage exported files, device backups and shared copies using the destination service or device settings. Recovery from an unreadable workshop preserves a copy of the original file in the app's local storage before replacing it with a valid backup.

Support website

The XyloQuote support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security, including visits made without signing in. See GitHub’s Pages documentation.

These two pages contain no analytics, scripts or forms. This website logging is separate from the app’s on-device workshop storage. For GitHub’s handling of website data, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Support and beta feedback

If you contact team@tsmdigital.com, the publisher receives the contact details and information you choose to send for responding to your request. Support messages are handled through the email services used by you and the publisher. Avoid sending customer details or a full workshop backup unless needed for a specific support issue. Support correspondence may remain in the publisher's email service and backups. You can request deletion of that correspondence through team@tsmdigital.com.

If you participate through TestFlight, Apple handles distribution and may provide the developer with test usage, crash information and feedback under its TestFlight practices. This information helps investigate reported problems and improve the beta. Screenshots or attachments you submit can contain workshop or customer information. TestFlight feedback remains subject to Apple's service controls; contact team@tsmdigital.com about information you submit to the publisher. See Apple's TestFlight information.

Changes and contact

This notice will be updated when the app's data handling changes. The current app has no subscription billing or publisher-hosted sync service. Contact team@tsmdigital.com with questions about this notice or information you have provided directly to the publisher.
