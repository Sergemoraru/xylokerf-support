SUPPORT

Support for your workshop.

Help with shop setup, customer quotes, actual costs and your saved workshop.

Prepare a quote

Set your currency, hourly cost, overhead and target margin in your shop profile. These are your own cost assumptions, not recommended market prices. Existing jobs keep their saved currency and rates when you update your shop defaults.

Create a job, add the customer's details and scope, then enter estimated materials, labor and other costs. Check the pricing breakdown, save a quote revision, and share its PDF. Review the PDF before sending it to a customer. Descriptions and scope are customer-facing; internal unit costs, cost notes and profit are excluded.

Record acceptance and job profit

After a customer approves a quote outside XyloQuote, open that revision and record their acceptance. This keeps that revision's price and overhead rate as the profit basis even if you save another estimate. It does not send a message, collect a signature or take payment.

Start work and record actual costs. Complete the job when the costs are entered, or reopen it when something changes. Profit excludes sales tax and depends on the costs you recorded; it does not show whether the customer has paid. Jobs without recorded acceptance show a provisional comparison. Sample jobs are excluded from portfolio totals.

Reuse costs

Save a rate or group of costs in Library, or save a job's estimated costs as a template. Templates can be applied to jobs in the same currency. Each use creates a separate copy, so changing a template later does not change an existing job or saved quote.

Duplicate a job to reuse its scope and estimate for new work. The copy starts as a fresh draft without the original customer, actual costs or quote/acceptance history.

Move work from XyloKerf

Export a cut-plan CSV from XyloKerf and import it into XyloQuote. Review the currency and missing prices. The import prices used Sheet/Board stock; finished parts, unused inventory and waste summaries are not charged again. Add labor, finishing, installation and other costs separately. The apps do not currently synchronize automatically or share a subscription.

Back up your workshop

In My shop, choose Export workshop backup and save the JSON file to a location you control, such as Files or iCloud Drive. It includes customer details and private costs; it is a full backup, not a customer quote.

Import workshop backup adds missing jobs and templates. Existing records with the same identifiers and your local shop settings are retained; ordinary import does not overwrite a job with another version of that job. There is no automatic cross-device sync. Export an up-to-date backup before changing devices or removing the app.

If the saved workshop cannot be opened, XyloQuote keeps it read-only. Import a valid backup to recover. Recovery preserves a local copy of the unreadable original before replacing it. Do not delete the app as the first troubleshooting step; removing local data can remove the workshop you are trying to recover.

Report a problem

Contact team@tsmdigital.com with the app version shown in My shop, your iPhone/iPad model, OS version, what you tried, and what happened. A screenshot with customer information hidden is helpful. Do not send a full workshop backup or customer records unless requested for a specific issue and you choose to share them.

For TestFlight builds, you can also submit feedback through TestFlight. The beta may expire; keep your own workshop backup. Check TestFlight for available updates and contact team@tsmdigital.com if you need help accessing your work.

Availability and purchases

The current beta runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. It works locally without an account. The beta has no paywall and does not charge a subscription. Pricing and paid features for a future release have not been finalized.

Supported currencies are USD, CAD, GBP, EUR and AUD. There is no currency conversion. Tax uses the rate you enter for the entire selling price; XyloQuote does not determine tax obligations or file taxes.
